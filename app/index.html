Read this before you start

  • One trophy may be missable: Positive Mental Attitude. In Chapter 1, one morning at Jason’s house, Lucia walks out to the balcony to meditate. Follow her and accept the optional task — it is gone once Chapter 1 ends. Keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.
  • There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 manual slots plus one autosave) and one untouched save from just before the final mission.
  • Cheats switch trophies off — and stop the game saving — so keep them off your trophy save.
  • Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 is time-sensitive (hourglass icon) and counts toward Strangers & Freaks: do it the moment it appears.

Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, in three stages. The story takes about 60 to 80 hours; the 100% cleanup takes about 100 more. Sources: PowerPyx and Push Square, as reported by Sixshore and Output Lag.

  1. Stage 1 — Story and side strands about 60–80 hours

    Play the story at your own pace. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, take optional missions as they appear (100% needs any 100 of them), scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app, study new animals through the binoculars, and turn on progress and criminal profile notifications in the UI settings so you can see what counts.

  2. Stage 2 — Free-roam cleanup the bulk of the list

    After the story, work through the robberies, activities, collectibles and exploration trophies. Bank vaults, jewelry stores, drone races, shooting ranges, cards, WAiNK spots and the 100% checklist items all stay open.

  3. Stage 3 — 100% and the platinum about 100 hours

    Finish the 100% checklist (A Long Road, gold), complete the last Strangers & Freaks strands, and the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, follows.
